## Line-Level Feedback
### Thesis clarity
- **Location**: TL;DR and paragraph 1 of "What is Omnigent?"
- **Issue**: The TL;DR delivers the thesis well. The opening of the main body takes a few sentences to set up context before stating the deployment goal.
- **Suggestion**: No major change needed. The TL;DR handles the "what's in it for me" question immediately.

### Opening hook
- **Location**: "What is Omnigent?" first paragraph
- **Issue**: "If you're managing multiple AI coding agents, you already know the pain" is a solid hook that identifies a gap. Could be slightly sharper by naming a specific friction point.
- **Suggestion**: Consider adding one concrete example of the integration pain (e.g., "rewriting your webhook handler for the third time this quarter").

### Closing strength
- **Location**: "Try it yourself" section
- **Issue**: The CTA is natural and links to the right resources. Slightly formulaic in structure (three links in sequence).
- **Suggestion**: Could tie back to the opening pain point for a more satisfying arc. Minor improvement.

## Summary
The structure is strong. H2s form a clear progression from problem to solution to lessons. The most impactful improvement would be sharpening the opening hook with a more specific friction example.
